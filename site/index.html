About

I am a consultant radiologist with a subspecialty focus in oncologic and head & neck imaging. I report CT, MRI, ultrasound, X-ray, fluoroscopy and mammography, and I currently report for more than 30 NHS hospitals with Medica Group. I am licensed in the UK, Germany, Luxembourg and India. I also advise medtech, pharma, consulting and investment firms.

I am a consultant diagnostic radiologist licensed in the United Kingdom, Germany, Luxembourg and India. I hold the German specialist qualification in radiology (Facharzt Radiologie), and I work across all the major modalities: CT, MRI, ultrasound, plain film, fluoroscopy and mammography. My subspecialty focus is oncologic imaging (tumour staging, treatment response and surveillance) and head & neck imaging, in both emergency and elective settings.

Since August 2026 I have been a consultant radiologist with Medica Group, reporting for more than 30 NHS hospitals. Before that I was a consultant radiologist at Saravana Heart Hospital in Chennai, where I contributed to and led multidisciplinary team meetings. I also spent nearly three years at Klinikum Mutterhaus in Trier, a hospital of more than 1,000 beds serving a major regional oncology centre. There I performed interventional procedures, including biopsies, stent placements, TACE and TIPS.

My path into radiology included haemostaseology and transfusion medicine at Saarland University Hospital, where I lectured medical students and developed software to help identify antibodies against red blood cells. In 2021 I received the German State of Saarland's highest medical award for improving hospital drug safety.

Alongside my clinical work, I have advised medtech, pharmaceutical, consulting and investment firms since 2022. I draw on a background that combines medicine and computer science.

Outside work, I enjoy guitar and music, meditation, cardiovascular and team sports, skiing and time in nature.
